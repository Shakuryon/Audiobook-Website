Where Could You Be Losing Opportunities?

Someone interested in visiting may have basic questions: service times, location, what to expect, children's ministry, accessibility, livestreams, and who to contact. If those answers are hard to find, the first step becomes harder.

Potential Revenue Leaks

  • Service times, directions, and contact details are buried in social posts.
  • New visitors cannot tell what to expect or how to get involved.
  • Ministry information, events, sermons, and giving links are spread across multiple platforms.

What Your Website Can Do

  • A clear first-time visitor page with service times, directions, parking, and what to expect.
  • Ministry pages for children, youth, outreach, groups, and other active programs.
  • Sermon, livestream, event, and announcement links.
  • A secure giving link through a trusted payment provider, plus clear contact options.

More Than a Website. A Better Customer Journey.

A website supports communication and accessibility; it does not replace in-person relationships, pastoral care, or community outreach. Online giving should use a reputable payment provider.

Giving links should send visitors to a secure third-party provider. Do not collect or store payment card details directly on a basic website.
