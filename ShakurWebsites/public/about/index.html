About Shakuryon

Evenin'! Friends call me Shak!

I'm Shakuryon "Shak" Steward! Web Developer, Entrepreneur, Crisis Counselor, Voice Actor, Singer, Charcoal Portrait Artist, Home Cook, Former FBI Special Agent Candidate, and most importantly, Lover of Shrimp Tacos. I have Hot-blooded cajun blood in my veins, and I am a proud Alabamian.

Early in my childhood, my family has always described me as very curious of learning new things, and having a knack of creating my imaginations in front of me instead of keeping them nestled in my cerebral cortex. It took one boring summer after my Freshman year of high school, to impulsively attempt to draw a portrait of Michael Jackson. It was not all that great, but the positive reinforcement that I received from others, inspired me to stick with creating art.

Which brings me to 3 years ago, while in my current mid-20's, where my long-term curiosity brought me to attending a Front-End/Back-End Bootcamp. After completion, I fell in love with pursuing web development as a full-time business. So after completing a Harvard University course certificate in "CS50 Advanced Python & Javascript" 6 months ago, I realized that I was ready for my new dream.
