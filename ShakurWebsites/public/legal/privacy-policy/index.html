Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

ShakurWebsites ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you may have regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request website services, or otherwise communicate with ShakurWebsites.

This information may include:

  • Your name or business name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Information about your business or website needs
  • Other information you choose to provide

We may also automatically receive limited technical information when you visit our website, such as browser type, device information, general usage information, and other information typically provided through standard web requests or analytics services.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information we collect may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Communicate with prospective or existing clients
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Build, maintain, or update websites for clients
  • Provide technical support
  • Monitor website performance and functionality
  • Protect our website and services from misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use information submitted through our contact forms for unrelated purposes without a legitimate reason or, where required, your consent.

4. Contact Forms & Communications

If you contact ShakurWebsites through a website contact form, email, telephone call, or another communication method, we may retain the information you provide in order to respond to your request and maintain appropriate business records.

Contact form submissions may be processed through third-party services used to deliver those submissions to us. Information submitted through a form should therefore be limited to information necessary for your inquiry.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, and maintain functionality.

Depending on the services implemented on the website, these technologies may collect information such as pages visited, approximate geographic information, browser type, device type, referral information, and general interaction with the website.

You may be able to control or disable certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers to support website functionality, hosting, communications, analytics, payments, forms, or other business operations.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any third-party service you interact with through our website.

7. Website Hosting

Websites designed or maintained by ShakurWebsites may be hosted using third-party hosting infrastructure.

Hosting providers may automatically process technical information associated with visitors, including IP addresses, browser information, device information, and server request information as part of providing and securing their hosting services.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information entrusted to us from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or through our website.

9. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information we maintain about you. These may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your request before completing it.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our website.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12. External Links

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
